### 2. `frappe-payments-comms` (`audit-payments` → `verifier`)
- **Audit result:** 14 verified findings (**2 Critical**, **4 High**, **8 Medium**); 24 static signals triaged (`23` `wontfix`, `1` escalated `CH-321`).

All 4 `sqli-candidate`, 5 `allow-guest-inventory`, and 14/15 `ignore-permissions-audit` signals verified safe/intentional.
- **Top risks:** Two Critical money-integrity defects: (1) unhandled ERPNext `Payment Entry` posting errors in `reconcile_and_post_square_payment()` roll back `square_payment_id` and strand the charged transaction in `Initiated` (`CH-467`); (2) retryable 408/5xx `SquareApiError` marks the transaction `Failed` instead of `Pending`, enabling double charges (`CH-468`). Also out-of-order Square webhook regressions (`CH-469`), unlocked lazy portal signing-secret init calling full `ISM Settings.save()` (`CH-470` / `CH-321`), TCPA `'OPT OUT'` / conversational-reply keyword mismatch (`CH-471`), and RFC1918/DNS SSRF in `is_valid_sms_webhook_url` (`CH-472`).
- **Cross-family verifier catches:** Flipped `CH-468` (`retryable-square-error-enables-double-charge`) to `safe_for_auto_fix: false` because returning HTTP 200 `Pending` without a `SquareClient.get_payment` reconciliation/expiry job would permanently strand ROs in `IN_FLIGHT_TX_STATUSES` on edge 502/503 errors where Square never created a payment; calibrated `CH-473` (`sms-opt-out-concurrent-first-insert-race`) from High → Medium and noted MariaDB deadlock (1213).
